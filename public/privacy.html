Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

1. Data Collection

Ease is a local-first application. All cubic-bezier calculations and animations are processed entirely in your browser using JavaScript. We do not collect, store, or transmit any data from your device.

2. No Server Processing

Unlike many online tools, Ease does not send your bezier coordinates or animation data to any server. All processing happens client-side, ensuring complete privacy.

3. No Cookies or Tracking

Ease does not use cookies, local storage, or any tracking technologies. We do not track your usage patterns or collect analytics data.

4. Third-Party Services

Ease uses Google Fonts for typography. These fonts are loaded directly from Google's servers. Google may collect anonymized usage data for font delivery optimization. You can review Google's privacy policy at their website.

5. Children's Privacy

Ease is suitable for users of all ages. Since we do not collect personal information, we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through Velocity Digital Architecture House at velocity.calyvent.com
